# Code of Conduct

AEIRO brings together people from many countries, disciplines and backgrounds. We are committed to making participation in our projects a respectful, professional and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

We expect everyone who takes part in AEIRO spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and events, to:

- Be respectful and considerate, even when disagreeing
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on evidence and on what is best for the work and the communities it serves
- Credit the work and ideas of others
- Respect the privacy of others and the confidentiality of shared data

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind
- Insulting, demeaning or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Knowingly sharing false or misleading data
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all AEIRO project spaces and when someone represents AEIRO in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to [info@aeiro.org](mailto:info@aeiro.org). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

AEIRO maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on the situation, they may issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict a person's participation.
