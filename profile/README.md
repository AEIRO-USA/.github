## Who we are

AEIRO is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit research organization working to accelerate Africa's energy transformation through rigorous scientific and engineering research. We study the **root causes** of energy-delivery challenges using power-system analysis, engineering modeling, system dynamics, data science, geospatial analysis, capacity planning and energy economics, and turn what we find into accurate, accessible and actionable insights.

We work alongside governments, electric utilities, industry, universities, researchers and communities to bridge the gap between scientific understanding and real-world implementation.

> **Our vision:** an Africa where abundant, reliable and sustainable energy powers thriving economies, transforms communities and unlocks opportunity for generations to come.

## The problem we're working on

Reliable electricity remains out of reach for much of Africa, despite decades of investment.

- **About 600 million people** in Africa still lack access to electricity.
- **$30–50 billion** is spent each year across African households and businesses on petrol and diesel generators.
- Public discussion of the causes is often not grounded in evidence, which leads to costly decisions that don't fix the underlying problems.

We believe the right solutions start with a correct, evidence-based understanding of why energy delivery fails.

## What we do

| Pillar | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Research & Insights** | Scientific and engineering research, modeling and energy data intelligence that clarify Africa's energy-delivery challenges. |
| **Technical Advisory & Planning** | Pro-bono utility support, network planning, investment prioritization and decision-support tools that turn evidence into better infrastructure choices. |
| **Capacity Building & Project Support** | Training, partnerships, utility capability development and demonstration projects that build lasting local technical capacity. |

**Focus areas:** electricity delivery is our initial focus, including the role of renewable energy and of oil and gas in electricity supply.

## How we work

**Research** → **Organize insights** → **Pilot solutions** → **Launch ventures**

We investigate problems, organize what we learn into trusted, accessible information, test solutions at small scale, and scale up the ones that work.

## Current work

### Project Lumina
Our inaugural research project, focused on a major geographical area in Nigeria. It builds a representative model of existing electricity infrastructure (generation, transmission and distribution, major industrial and residential loads, microgrids and local generation, and cost patterns) and uses system dynamics to understand the complexity of electricity delivery. The aim is a set of actionable projects and improvement programs.

### AEIRO Data Hub *(pilot in development)*
A platform for reliable, up-to-date energy data and insights across Africa, with interactive datasets, visualizations and research tools for policymakers, utilities, investors and communities. [Join the early testers' group](https://aeiro.org/support/).
