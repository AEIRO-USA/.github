## About AEIRO

The Africa Energy Information Research Organization (AEIRO) is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit research organization. We study the **root causes** of Africa's energy-delivery challenges and turn the findings into accurate, accessible and actionable insights for the people who plan, finance and operate energy systems.

Our methods include power-system analysis, engineering modeling, system dynamics, data science, geospatial analysis, capacity planning and energy economics. We work alongside governments, electric utilities, industry, universities, researchers and communities to bridge the gap between scientific understanding and real-world implementation.

> **Our vision:** an Africa where abundant, reliable and sustainable energy powers thriving economies, transforms communities and unlocks opportunity for generations to come.

## The challenge

Reliable electricity remains out of reach for much of Africa, despite decades of investment.

- **About 600 million** people in Africa lack access to electricity.
- **$30–50 billion** is spent each year by African households and businesses on petrol and diesel generators.

Too often, decisions about energy infrastructure are made without a clear, evidence-based understanding of why delivery fails. AEIRO exists to provide that understanding.

## What we do

| Pillar | Focus |
|:--|:--|
| **Research & Insights** | Scientific and engineering research, modeling and energy data intelligence that clarify Africa's energy-delivery challenges |
| **Technical Advisory & Planning** | Pro bono utility support, network planning, investment prioritization and decision-support tools for better infrastructure choices |
| **Capacity Building & Project Support** | Training, partnerships, utility capability development and demonstration projects that build lasting local expertise |

**Focus areas:** electricity delivery is our initial focus, including the role of renewable energy and of oil and gas in electricity supply.

**Our approach:** Research → Organize insights → Pilot solutions → Launch ventures

## Current work

**Project Lumina**<br>
Our inaugural research project, focused on a major region of Nigeria. It builds a representative model of existing electricity infrastructure, covering generation, transmission and distribution, major industrial and residential loads, microgrids and local generation, and cost patterns. It uses system dynamics to understand what drives electricity-delivery outcomes, with the aim of defining actionable improvement programs.

**AEIRO Data Hub** · *pilot in development*<br>
A platform for reliable, up-to-date energy data across Africa, with interactive datasets, visualizations and research tools for policymakers, utilities, investors and communities. [Join the early testers' group →](https://aeiro.org/support/)
